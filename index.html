Beyond the Savings, Here's Why Turkey Makes Sense

Strategically located

Turkey sits between Europe and Asia, with direct flights to Lagos and Abuja and a large, established Nigerian and African student community already in Istanbul.

English-taught programmes

No requirement to learn Turkish first. Most programmes in this pathway are taught fully in English.

Work while you study

International students in Turkey have access to part-time work opportunities to help offset living costs.

A straightforward visa process

Compared to the UK, US, or Canada, Turkey's student visa process has fewer discretionary refusal points and a clearer document checklist.

You land into a support system, not a void

A welcome team meets you at Istanbul airport, and group accommodation options are arranged in advance, so you're not figuring out housing alone in a new country.
